## WAVE 2 RISK ASSESSMENT

### High Risks (Mitigation Required)

**Risk 1: Scaling Issues**
- Problem: System degrades with 4x patient load
- Mitigation: Database stress testing done, redundancy ready
- Contingency: Fall back to 2 units if needed

**Risk 2: Staff Training Gaps**
- Problem: New staff not competent despite training
- Mitigation: Competency assessments required, mentorship assigned
- Contingency: Intensive 1-on-1 coaching available

**Risk 3: Medium-Confidence Forecast Misuse**
- Problem: Staff over-rely on 60-70% confidence forecasts
- Mitigation: Training emphasizes verification, alerts in dashboard
- Contingency: Manual review procedures enforce

### Medium Risks

**Risk 4: Data Quality Varies by Unit**
- Problem: Unit 3-4 have lower quality vital measurements
- Mitigation: Data quality validation procedures, staff education
- Contingency: Adjust confidence thresholds per unit

**Risk 5: Clinician Burnout from Alerts**
- Problem: Too many alerts overwhelm clinicians
- Mitigation: Dynamic threshold tuning, feedback collection
- Contingency: Reduce alert sensitivity if needed

### Low Risks

**Risk 6: Infrastructure Outages**
- Problem: Network or power failure in Unit 2-3
- Mitigation: UPS installed, failover procedures tested
- Contingency: Fallback to manual monitoring
