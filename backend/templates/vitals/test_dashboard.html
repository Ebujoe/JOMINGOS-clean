💡 How the System Works

NEWS2 Scoring: Each vital sign is scored (0-3 points), and the total determines risk level.

  • GREEN (0-4): Low risk - routine monitoring
  • YELLOW (5-6): Medium risk - increased monitoring
  • HIGH (7+): Critical - immediate review required

Alerts: Automatically triggered when NEWS2 ≥ 7 or significant deterioration trends detected.
