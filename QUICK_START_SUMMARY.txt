HOW IT WORKS (SIMPLE)
================================================================================

STEP 1: Record Vitals
  → Patient health data captured (temperature, heart rate, oxygen, etc.)
  → Stored in VitalSigns table

STEP 2: Generate Forecast
  → System analyzes last 3-10 vital readings
  → Three independent models make predictions:
    • Linear regression (continuation of trend)
    • Exponential smoothing (weighted recent)
    • Moving average (smoothed projection)
  → Models combined via ensemble weighting

STEP 3: Calculate Risk
  → Compare forecasted vitals to clinical thresholds
  → Calculate hours until critical (if applicable)
  → Determine trajectory level and urgency
  → Generate recommendations

STEP 4: Store & Display
  → Save prediction in PredictiveRiskAssessment table
  → Display in dashboard and detail pages
  → Make available via API

STEP 5: Clinical Action
  → Staff reviews dashboard
  → Sees time-to-critical forecast
  → Takes early action based on recommendations

================================================================================
FORECASTING MODELS (THE MATH)
================================================================================

LINEAR REGRESSION:
  • Fits straight line through recent vitals
  • Formula: y = mx + b (where m is slope, b is intercept)
  • Good for: Steady trends
  • Risk: Assumes trend continues unchanged

EXPONENTIAL SMOOTHING (α=0.3):
  • Weighted average: (0.3 × current) + (0.7 × previous forecast)
  • Good for: Catching recent changes
  • Risk: Can overreact to single readings

MOVING AVERAGE (3-point):
  • Average of last 3 readings projected forward
  • Good for: Noise reduction
  • Risk: Slower to detect real changes

ENSEMBLE COMBINATION:
  • Weight each model by data stability
  • High variance → lower weight
  • Low variance → higher weight
  • Final prediction = weighted average

CRITICAL THRESHOLDS
================================================================================

                    CRITICAL        WARNING        NORMAL
Heart Rate          <40 / >130      <50 / >110     50-110 bpm
Respiratory Rate    <8 / >30        <10 / >24      10-24 br/min
Oxygen Saturation   <85%            <90%           ≥90%
Temperature         <35 / >39       <36 / >38      36-38°C
BP Systolic         <90 / >180      <100 / >160    100-160 mmHg

When forecast breaches CRITICAL threshold:
  → Calculate hours until breach
  → Assign CRITICAL risk level
  → Alert clinical staff

RISK LEVELS
================================================================================

🟢 STABLE
   └─ No critical vitals predicted
      Action: Continue routine monitoring

🟡 SLOW DETERIORATION
   └─ Critical projected in 48-72 hours
      Action: Plan for escalation

🟠 MODERATE DETERIORATION
   └─ Critical projected in 24-48 hours
      Action: Increase monitoring frequency

🔴 RAPID DETERIORATION
   └─ Critical projected in 6-24 hours
      Action: Prepare intervention

🔴🔴 CRITICAL WITHIN 24H
   └─ Critical projected in <6 hours
      Action: IMMEDIATE intervention
